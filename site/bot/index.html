NoahsArchContactBot

User-agent: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; NoahsArchContactBot/1.0; +https://agents.noahsarch.tech/bot)

This fetcher belongs to the Website Contact & Social Extractor. It only fetches a page when a customer explicitly asks for that website's published contact details — typically the homepage and its contact / about page, at most 20 pages per request, 2 MB each, with a 10-second timeout. It is not a crawler: it does not follow links beyond the site's own contact pages, does not index, does not run JavaScript, does not use proxies, and does not retry blocked requests.

It respects

  • robots.txt rules for NoahsArchContactBot and *
  • HTTP 403/429 — reported to the customer, never circumvented
  • Private networks — it refuses to connect to non-public addresses

To block it

User-agent: NoahsArchContactBot
Disallow: /
